REASONS NOT TO HIRE ME


If you want someone who just says yes — I am not that person.
I will rename your variables. I can't help it. Bad names physically pain me.
I need to understand why before I commit to how. This slows things down upfront.
I have opinions about process. Strong ones. I'll share them once and then let it go. Mostly.
I read too many books and will occasionally reference them in Slack.
I don't do well with "we've always done it this way" as a full justification.
I will ask for a meeting agenda before attending a meeting with no agenda.
